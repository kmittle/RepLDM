## Common Reproduction Rules

All arms use the frozen SDXL U-Net/VAE, fixed CFG, resolution, timestep list,
initial-noise seed, prompt-disjoint manifests, and one device per paired block.
“Matched compute” means actual U-Net evaluations plus feature hooks, backward
passes, FFTs, reward calls, and wall time, not nominal denoising-loop length.
Action order is randomized and recorded. Priors, detectors, EMA thresholds,
energy schedules, guidance step sizes, and reward weights are frozen before the
held-out split. Any extra U-Net call is an explicitly labelled upper bound.

The defensible journal claim remains the causal combination of a low-capacity,
structure-conditioned residual basis and scheduler-consistent bounded
injection. It cannot be expanded to “first latent renderer,” “first frozen
internal intervention,” “first Riemannian or spectral guidance,” “first
mean-preserving/noise guidance,” “first trajectory correction,” or “first
SDE-consistent diffusion-RL sampler.” A fixed FRLA action must beat these
controls and no-op on held-out quality/structure metrics before search,
distillation, or RL is allowed; otherwise report the closest simpler method.
